[Global heuristics on druggability and synthesizability]
  1. Synthesizability is a hard constraint: any edit that drastically
     reduces SA is rejected.
  2. Avoid forcing exotic bonds (e.g., N-O, N-F) through
     `pharmacophore_smarts`.
  3. Prefer empty `pharmacophore_smarts` and rely on semantic constraints
     to retrieve stable fragments.
